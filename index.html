About Keegan

Keegan McCarthy has spent more than fifteen years serving in local church ministry, with experience spanning worship, youth ministry, and production. Much of that time has been spent serving alongside volunteers and helping people grow in both confidence and skill.

Getting Out of the Way grew out of those years of serving, learning, and teaching in the local church.

Keegan lives in Central Ohio with his wife, Allie, and their two sons, Lincoln and Theo.
